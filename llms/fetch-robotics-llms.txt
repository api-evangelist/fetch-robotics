# Fetch Robotics

> Fetch Robotics is a provider of autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) for manufacturing, distribution, and fulfillment. Its robots (Fetch, Freight) are programmed through a ROS (Robot Operating System) interface rather than an HTTP/REST API. The company was acquired by Zebra Technologies in August 2021; fetchrobotics.com now redirects to Zebra and the autonomous-mobile-robot line is being wound down (2025-2026). The open-source ROS packages remain published in the fetchrobotics GitHub organization.

## Overview
- Programmatic surface: ROS (Robot Operating System) topics, services, and actions — not an HTTP/REST API.
- Standard interfaces: control_msgs/FollowJointTrajectory (arm/torso), control_msgs/PointHead (head), geometry_msgs/Twist (mobile base), sensor_msgs/Imu (IMU sensors).
- Conforms to ROS Enhancement Proposals (REPs) for code portability across ROS robots.
